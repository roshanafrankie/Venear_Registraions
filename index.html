1. Project Context & Objectives

Resilient Plywood-Based Veneer Construction in Sri Lanka

The proposed buildings adopt an improved plywood-based construction technology introduced with the technical support of a Japanese Expert Team, with particular emphasis on rapid, practical and resilient construction.

In addition to supporting the planned construction activities, the initiative seeks to transfer and disseminate knowledge related to Veneer House construction among Sri Lankan contractors, strengthening local construction capacity in disaster-affected and disaster-prone areas where rapid construction of community facilities and other essential buildings may be required after disasters and emergencies.

Important Pre-condition

Only contractors who successfully register for and participate in the training programme will be eligible to participate in the subsequent bidding/procurement process for the Veneer House construction works, subject to the eligibility, qualification and other requirements specified in the formal solicitation documents issued by UNDP.

Key Highlights

  • ✓ Improved Plywood-Based Technology: Improved plywood-based construction introduced with the technical support of a Japanese Expert Team.
  • ✓ Rapid, Practical & Resilient: Construction approach designed for speed, practicality and resilience.
  • ✓ 18 Veneer Houses: Planned at selected locations in the Central Province, in two stages.
  • ✓ Knowledge Transfer: Practical knowledge shared with Sri Lankan contractors to strengthen local capacity.
2. Mandatory Requirement

Specialized Veneer House Training Programme

UNDP will conduct a specialized one-day Training Programme on Veneer House Construction under the technical guidance of a Japanese Expert Team. All contractors intending to participate in the subsequent procurement process for the construction of Veneer Houses are required to participate.

Technical & Practical Guidance Covers

• Veneer House construction methodology, construction sequence and key construction techniques.

• Material requirements and relevant workmanship considerations.
• Relevant aspects of the Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and implementation requirements.
3. Construction Plan

18 Veneer Houses in Two Stages

Stage 1
6 Houses

Two (2) units in each of the Kandy, Matale and Nuwara Eliya Districts.

Stage 2
12 Houses

Four (4) units in each of the Kandy, Matale and Nuwara Eliya Districts.

The works will be procured through a competitive national bidding process, expected to commence after the Veneer House Construction Training Programme. Bidding requirements, construction packages/lots, eligibility criteria and contractual conditions will be specified in the solicitation documents issued by UNDP.
