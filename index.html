1. Project Context & Objectives

Resilient Plywood-Based Veneer Construction in Sri Lanka

The proposed buildings adopt an improved plywood-based construction technology introduced with the technical support of a renowned Japanese Expert Team. The system emphasizes rapid, practical, and disaster-resilient construction techniques tailored for post-emergency recovery.

In addition to delivering 18 community facilities, this initiative seeks to transfer and disseminate knowledge and practical skills to Sri Lankan local contractors, substantially upgrading community infrastructure response capacities.

Important Pre-condition

Only contractors who successfully register for and complete the mandatory one-day training programme will be eligible to participate in the subsequent national competitive bidding process for these works.

Key Highlights of Veneer Technology

  • ✓ Pre-engineered Timber Joinery: Structural interlocking with Thin-Gan hardwood footings & base anchors.
  • ✓ Rapid Erection: Modular prefabricated panels allow completion in a fraction of conventional masonry timelines.
  • ✓ Disaster Resistance: Lightweight, flexible frame ideal for seismic stability and extreme monsoon climates.
  • ✓ Direct Mentorship: On-site guidance provided by the Japanese Expert Team and UNDP Senior Engineers.
